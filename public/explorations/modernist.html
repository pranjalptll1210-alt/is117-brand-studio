    DESIGN NOTES / A / Modernist

    Ordered after dark

    Reference inspected
    Josef Müller-Brockmann, Beethoven concert poster, 1955 ↗

    A large geometric field carries the visual energy while compact sans-serif information stays grouped.

    Design choices
    A two-column grid separates expression from shopping. A single sans-serif family and consistent alignment create hierarchy. A restrained burgundy circle translates the poster’s geometric rhythm into a product stage.
    Intended effect (hypothesis)
    The primary visitor gets a confident identity and a direct route to the shirt; the secondary visitor can inspect an uncluttered product image.
    Risk to review
    The order may feel too controlled for a visitor seeking a more disruptive streetwear identity.

    Both previews use identical product content and the original shirt asset. These are proposals; no final direction or logo has been selected.
