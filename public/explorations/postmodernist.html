    DESIGN NOTES / B / Postmodernist

    A different rhythm

    Reference inspected
    Wolfgang Weingart, Schreibkunst (The Art of Writing), 1981 ↗

    Overlapping halftone fields, angular shapes, and differently scaled type create several visual layers.

    Design choices
    An offset product plate and angled burgundy band quote print collage. Sans-serif and italic serif headline fragments mix visual languages. A dotted field exposes the texture of printed imagery; controls remain on a stable horizontal grid.
    Intended effect (hypothesis)
    The primary visitor encounters a more individual, late-night editorial attitude; the secondary visitor still gets the same clear product image and controls.
    Risk to review
    The layered composition may compete with the product or feel less effortless; mobile reduces the decorative overlap.

    Both previews use identical product content and the original shirt asset. These are proposals; no final direction or logo has been selected.
